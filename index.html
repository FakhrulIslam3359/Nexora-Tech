Wireless Earbuds: What to Compare

Before choosing earbuds, check comfort, sound preferences, microphone quality, battery life, charging case, device compatibility, return policy, and warranty. Features and performance vary by model, so verify the manufacturer's specifications before purchasing.

Smartwatches: Important Features

Check whether the watch works with your phone, which notifications it supports, how long the battery lasts, and whether its apps meet your needs. Read the manufacturer's water-resistance guidance before using a watch around water.

Chargers: Safety and Compatibility

Check the required connector, supported charging standard, power output, and device manufacturer's recommendations. Choose products from reputable sellers and verify applicable safety certifications.
